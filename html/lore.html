Dive into The Borderlands

Porubizhzhia is a land that once knew how to live in a fragile accord with nature, spirits, and ancient powers. Now it lies defiled by centuries of bloodshed: its fields are strewn with bodies, its forests cut down, and its waters poisoned by death. The beings with whom humanity once coexisted in a perilous yet understood peace have begun to reclaim their place in this world. The deep forests no longer let those who enter their depths return. The black waters drown any who dare cross them. The mountains conceal their paths and send stone crashing beneath the feet of those who seek to conquer their peaks.
The Old Order has fallen. In its place rose the Sunfaith — a religion that promises humanity light, protection, and the right to live without fear of unclean forces. It teaches that humankind deserves a place of its own in this world, unshared with spirits, demons, and ancient horrors. Thus began a holy war between the remnants of the Old Order, the new faith, and the land itself, which no longer wishes to submit to man.
But in Porubizhzhia, the line between light and darkness was erased long ago. Not every shadow is evil. Not every light brings salvation. And perhaps the most terrible question in this world is not what lurks in the dark — but what is hidden behind the blinding light.
