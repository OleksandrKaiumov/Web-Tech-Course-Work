GLOSSARY

The Old Order
The Old Order is an ancient pagan faith of the Porubizhzhia, rooted in the veneration of spirits, beings, dead ancestors, and the living force of the nature. According to this belief, the world never belonged to humankind alone: forests, waters, fields, mountains, boundaries, and homes all have their own masters, guardians, or inhabitants, and one must live with them carefully-through ritual, sacrifice, taboo, and remembrance. The Old Order teaches how to discern whom to appease, whom to avoid, whom to fear, and with whom a pact may be made.
The Sunfaith
The Sunfaith is a new, radiant religion that promises people protection, purification, and the right to live without fear of unclean forces. It teaches that the world must be ordered according to human will, and that all things dark, wild, and otherworldly must be either subdued or destroyed. For some, the Sunfaith has become a source of salvation and support; for others, it is a force that violently rewrites the Old Order, severing the ancient bonds between humankind and the living world.
The Bright-faced
The Bright-Faced are the most elite warriors of the Sunfaith, the living instruments of the Patriarch’s will, sent wherever ordinary armies do not survive. Publicly, they are said to be those who have renounced their own names, bloodlines, and former lives in the service of the Sun. Among the common folk, however, the Bright-Faced are feared almost as much as the unclean itself: for their masks, their cold demeanor, their inhuman endurance, and the sense that beneath the radiance of their faces lurks something dark and unspoken.
